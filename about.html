About the Doctor

Dr. S. Sureshbhalaji

Consultant Urologist & Andrologist — experienced surgical care with a patient-first approach.

Profile

Experienced care, explained clearly.

Dr. S. Sureshbhalaji is a urologist and andrologist with expertise across kidney stone surgery, laser procedures, prostate surgery, laparoscopic surgery and renal transplantation.

With more than 800 renal transplantation cases over 10 years and extensive experience in kidney stone surgery — including laser kidney stone removal and prostate laser surgery — he brings depth of surgical experience to both common and complex urological conditions.

As an andrologist and male infertility specialist, he provides thoughtful evaluation and treatment options tailored to each patient's needs.
