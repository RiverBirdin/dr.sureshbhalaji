About the Doctor

Experienced hands. Clear guidance. Personal care.

Dr. S. Sureshbhalaji is a urologist and andrologist with expertise across kidney stone surgery, laser procedures, prostate surgery, laparoscopic surgery and renal transplantation.

His approach combines surgical experience with a simple principle: patients should understand their condition, their treatment options and the next step in their care.
